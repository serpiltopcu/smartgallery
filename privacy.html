Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Smart Gallery: Photo Cleaner does not collect, store, or share personal data by default. Photo and video processing is performed entirely on your device. The optional "Email Cleaner" feature requires access to your Google account only when you explicitly choose to connect Gmail.

  • We do not upload your photo or video files to our servers.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, or other identifying information unless you use the Email Cleaner feature.
  • Google account data is accessed only after you explicitly authorize the Gmail integration.

2. Photo Library Access

The app requires access to your photo library to detect duplicate or similar photos and videos, compress videos, and help you clean your gallery. This processing takes place on your device. Photos and videos are not uploaded to our servers.

3. Face Detection & Grouping

The app uses on-device face detection to automatically group similar portraits. Face detection and grouping are performed entirely on your device. Face data and biometric information are not uploaded to our servers, stored by us, or shared with third parties.

4. Gmail Integration (Email Cleaner)

The app offers an optional "Email Cleaner" feature. This feature is activated only when you explicitly choose to connect a Gmail account. Authentication is performed through Google's official OAuth authentication system. We never see, collect, or store your Gmail password.

  • We access the connected account's email address, message metadata such as sender, subject, date, and Gmail category labels (including Social, Promotions, Updates, and Forums). We do not intentionally read or process the body/content of your emails.
  • Gmail data is used only to provide the Email Cleaner functionality you explicitly request, including counting and listing emails by category, moving emails you select to Gmail's Trash, and opening unsubscribe links.
  • The Gmail data accessed by Smart Gallery is processed locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, analyzes, or transfers your Gmail data.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally on your device using iOS Keychain secure storage. These credentials are not transmitted to Smart Gallery servers or shared with third parties.
  • We request the Gmail gmail.modify permission because the Email Cleaner feature must be able to move emails selected by the user to Gmail's Trash. A more limited read-only Gmail permission would not allow this user-initiated action. Smart Gallery does not use this permission to send emails, compose emails, modify Gmail settings, or manage Gmail labels.
  • Emails selected for deletion are moved to Gmail's Trash rather than being permanently deleted by Smart Gallery. Gmail controls the retention and permanent deletion of messages in Trash according to Google's applicable Gmail policies.
  • Gmail user data is retained only for as long as it is necessary to provide the Email Cleaner feature. When you disconnect your Gmail account from Smart Gallery, the locally stored OAuth credentials for that account are deleted from the device and the app no longer has access to that account through the stored credentials.
  • You may also revoke Smart Gallery's access at any time through your Google Account permissions page. After access is revoked, Smart Gallery can no longer use the revoked authorization.
Smart Gallery handles Gmail user data in accordance with Google's API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Google User Data Protection, Retention & Deletion

Smart Gallery applies technical and organizational measures designed to protect Google user data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

  • Gmail OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally in iOS Keychain and are protected by Apple's platform security mechanisms.
  • Smart Gallery does not operate a server that stores Gmail messages, Gmail message content, or Google user data.
  • Gmail message information retrieved by the app is used temporarily to provide the Email Cleaner interface and is not retained in a remote database.
  • Google user data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the Gmail functionality requested by the user.
  • When the user disconnects a Gmail account from Smart Gallery, the associated OAuth tokens stored by the app are deleted from the device.
  • The user can also revoke Smart Gallery's Google account access through their Google Account security settings. After access is revoked, the app can no longer access the Gmail account using the revoked credentials.
  • Messages moved to Gmail Trash by the user remain under Google's Gmail retention and deletion mechanisms. Smart Gallery does not permanently delete those messages from Google's systems.
  • Smart Gallery does not maintain an independent copy of Gmail messages after the Gmail cleaning operation is completed.

6. Purchases & Subscriptions

Smart Gallery offers an optional Premium yearly subscription. Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. We do not collect or store payment card information. Purchase and subscription information is managed by Apple and RevenueCat. RevenueCat may process information such as subscription status and purchase date to manage subscription entitlements.

RevenueCat privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Gmail API – used only when the user explicitly enables the Email Cleaner feature.
  • RevenueCat – subscription management and entitlement processing.
  • Apple App Store – payment processing and app distribution.
  • Sentry – crash and error reporting. Sentry may receive anonymous device and error information to help us diagnose technical issues. Photo files and Gmail content are not sent to Sentry.
No advertising or user-tracking SDKs are used by Smart Gallery.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page together with the updated date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our handling of Google user data, please contact us at: smartgalleryphoto@gmail.com

4. Gmail Entegrasyonu (E-posta Temizleyici)

Smart Gallery isteğe bağlı bir "E-posta Temizleyici" özelliği sunar. Gmail erişimi yalnızca Google hesabınızı Google'ın resmi OAuth kimlik doğrulama sistemi üzerinden bağlamayı açıkça seçtiğinizde etkinleştirilir. Smart Gallery Google şifrenizi almaz veya saklamaz.

  • Bağladığınız Google hesabının e-posta adresine, gönderen, konu ve tarih gibi mesaj başlıklarına ve Sosyal, Promosyonlar, Güncellemeler ve Forumlar gibi Gmail kategori bilgilerine erişiriz.
  • Smart Gallery e-postalarınızın gövdesini/içeriğini bilerek okumaz veya saklamaz. E-posta içeriği sunucularımıza yüklenmez.
  • Gmail entegrasyonu için gerekli erişim ve yenileme tokenları cihaz üzerinde iOS Keychain kullanılarak saklanır. iOS Keychain, Apple'ın güvenli kimlik bilgisi saklama mekanizmasıdır.
  • OAuth tokenları işletim sisteminin erişim kontrolleri tarafından korunur ve Smart Gallery sunucularına gönderilmez veya sunucularımızda saklanmaz.
  • Gmail verileri yalnızca kullanıcının talep ettiği E-posta Temizleyici işlevini sağlamak için kullanılır. Buna mesajları listeleme ve sayma, mesaj bilgilerini görüntüleme, kullanıcının seçtiği mesajları Gmail Çöp Kutusu'na taşıma ve abonelikten çıkma bağlantılarını açma dahildir.
  • Google kullanıcı verileri reklam, kişiselleştirilmiş reklam, kredi değerliliği, kredi verme veya başka ilgisiz amaçlar için kullanılmaz.
  • Google kullanıcı verileri satılmaz, kiralanmaz veya üçüncü tarafların kendi amaçları için kullanılmak üzere paylaşılmaz.
  • Google kullanıcı verileri Gmail işleviyle ilgisiz analiz veya takip amacıyla kullanılmaz.
Smart Gallery'nin Gmail verilerini kullanması Google API Services User Data Policy ve Limited Use gerekliliklerine tabidir.
