Smart Gallery

Smart Gallery is a productivity application that helps users organize and clean their photo library and Gmail inbox. The app can detect duplicate and blurry photos, organize screenshots, identify subscription emails, help users unsubscribe from unwanted mailing lists, and reduce inbox clutter.

Download on the App Store

Application purpose

Smart Gallery is designed to help users organize and clean both their photo library and Gmail inbox. The application analyzes photo and email content in order to identify unnecessary items and improve organization.

Why Gmail access is required

Smart Gallery requests Gmail access only after the user explicitly signs in with Google and grants permission. Gmail access is used exclusively to identify subscription emails, analyze Gmail categories, help users unsubscribe from unwanted mailing lists, and move unwanted emails to the trash. Smart Gallery does not use Gmail data for advertising, marketing, or any purpose unrelated to the features requested by the user.

Gmail data is accessed solely to provide the inbox organization and subscription cleanup features requested by the user.

Main features

Duplicate photo detection

Find and remove duplicate photos quickly and safely.

Blurry photo cleanup

Identify low-quality images that can be deleted.

Screenshot organization

Organize screenshots and similar images automatically.

Gmail subscription management

Find newsletters and unsubscribe from unwanted mailing lists.

Inbox cleanup

Move unwanted emails to the trash and reduce inbox clutter.

Storage optimization

Free up device storage by removing unnecessary photos and emails.

Privacy and data use

Smart Gallery is committed to protecting user privacy.

  • Google sign-in is required before Gmail data can be accessed.
  • Gmail access is limited to the permissions granted by the user.
  • Gmail data is used only to provide inbox organization and subscription cleanup features.
  • Smart Gallery does not sell user data.
