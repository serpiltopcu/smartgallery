Smart Gallery

Smart Gallery helps users organize their photo library and Gmail inbox, remove duplicates, identify blurry photos, manage subscription emails, and free up storage space securely.

Download on the App Store

Application purpose

Smart Gallery is a productivity application designed to help users organize and clean both their photo library and Gmail inbox.

The application allows users to find duplicate and blurry photos, organize screenshots and similar images, free up device storage, analyze Gmail categories, identify subscription emails, unsubscribe from unwanted mailing lists, and move unwanted emails to the trash.

Gmail access is used solely to provide inbox organization and subscription cleanup features requested by the user.

Gmail access and privacy

Smart Gallery only accesses Gmail data after the user explicitly signs in with Google and grants permission.

The application reads Gmail categories and subscription information in order to help users identify newsletters and unsubscribe from unwanted emails.

Smart Gallery does not sell user data and only accesses the Gmail information necessary to provide the requested functionality.

Main features

Duplicate photo detection

Find and remove duplicate photos quickly.

Blurry photo cleanup

Identify low-quality images that can be deleted.

Gmail subscription cleanup

Find newsletters and unsubscribe from unwanted mailing lists.

Storage optimization

Free up space by removing unnecessary photos and emails.

Privacy and contact

All photo processing is performed on the device whenever possible. Gmail access is limited to the permissions explicitly granted by the user.
