What Smart Gallery does

Smart Gallery helps users manage large photo libraries by finding duplicate photos, blurry images, screenshots, and other unnecessary media. The app provides smart cleanup suggestions while keeping all photo analysis on the device.

Features

Duplicate detection

Quickly find and remove duplicate photos to free up valuable storage.

Blurry photo cleanup

Identify low-quality and blurry photos that can be safely deleted.

Smart organization

Group similar photos, screenshots, and media into organized categories.

Privacy-first

All photo processing happens locally on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to our servers.
