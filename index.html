A cleaner, more organized phone

Smart Gallery goes beyond photo cleanup. It helps you manage photos, videos and contacts in one simple place.

◫

Duplicate photos

Find duplicate and similar photos and review unnecessary copies quickly.

◉

Blurry photos

Identify potentially blurry or low-quality photos you may want to remove.

▣

Screenshots

Review screenshots separately and clean temporary images more easily.

▶

Video compression

Reduce the size of large videos and recover storage space.

👥

Contacts scan

Find contacts that share the same phone number or email and review them before merging.

✦

Smart organization

Review media and contacts through simple categories designed for faster cleanup.

Photo, video and contact analysis happens on your device. Smart Gallery does not upload your personal media or contacts to Smart Gallery servers.
