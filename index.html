A cleaner photo library

Smart Gallery helps you review and organize your iPhone photo library more efficiently. Instead of manually searching through thousands of photos, Smart Gallery helps identify content that may no longer be useful.

Your photos stay under your control. Smart Gallery is designed to help you review your library and decide what you want to keep or remove.

Main features

Tools designed to make cleaning and organizing your photo library easier.

◫

Duplicate photos

Find duplicate and similar photos so you can quickly review unnecessary copies.

◉

Blurry photos

Identify potentially blurry or low-quality photos that you may want to remove.

▣

Screenshots

Review screenshots separately and clean up temporary images more easily.

▶

Video compression

Reduce the size of large videos and recover storage space while keeping your important content.

✦

Smart organization

Review different types of media through simple categories designed for faster cleanup.

↓

Save storage

Remove unnecessary media and compress large files to free up space on your device.

Privacy

Smart Gallery is built with user privacy in mind.

  • Access to your photo library requires your permission.
  • You remain in control of the photos and videos you choose to delete.
  • Smart Gallery does not sell your personal data.
  • Permissions are used only to provide the features available in the application.
